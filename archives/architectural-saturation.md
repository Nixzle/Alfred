# Architectural Saturation Policy

Status: canonical Archive
Date: 2026-09-05

## Meaning

Sanctum may be considered **architecturally saturated** when additional broad donor sweeps predominantly rediscover capability classes already represented by an existing Member responsibility, Archive, Spellbook maneuver, or governed tool contract, and when newly discovered systems mostly differ in implementation details or operational scars rather than introducing a materially new capability class.

Architectural saturation does **not** mean internet completeness, finality, or permission to stop learning.

## Saturation test

A broad sweep is saturated when all of the following hold:

1. major agent/runtime primitives are represented: orchestration, research, memory, skills/procedures, tools/MCP/plugins/APIs, delegation, durable work, observability, evaluation, scope/authority, sandboxing, provider routing, scheduling/event wakeups and interoperability;
2. software-engineering/game-development donor disciplines are represented in Archives and retrieval policy;
3. reliability scars from multiple independent ecosystems are harvested, not merely headline features;
4. extension ecosystems, standards, marketplaces and portable context/skill layers are included in the donor map;
5. the anti-Hermes question is asked before declaring completeness: `What solves this problem without calling itself what I am searching for?`;
6. remaining high-value novelty is mainly implementation quality, evidence, security hardening, operational lessons, or a newly emerging external capability class;
7. broad searching has lower expected value than validating, integrating, or improving already adopted mechanisms.

## Post-saturation research policy

Once saturated, stop continuous random framework hunting. Reopen broad Cerebro/Mind Stone raids only when Prime Sense catches one of these triggers:

- a genuinely new protocol, ecosystem or marketplace becomes material;
- a recurring user correction reveals a missing source/capability class;
- an existing member repeatedly fails in a way current Archives/Spellbooks cannot explain;
- a new adjacent professional discipline appears relevant;
- a major platform/runtime/model change invalidates assumptions;
- community/maintainer evidence reveals a recurring production scar not covered by current doctrine;
- a new donor can delete substantial custom work or a trust boundary;
- Web/Watcher evidence shows our current implementation underperforms a proven external approach.

## Current verdict

After the September 5 peer-system, Hermes/plugin/MCP, sensing-discipline, engineering, anti-Hermes, Claw-family, A2A, portable-context, skill-registry, self-audit and cross-host-memory sweeps, Sanctum is **architecturally saturated at the capability-class level**.

This verdict is conditional and freshness-bounded. It means the current map is broad enough that the default should shift from `find more categories` to `validate, integrate, harden, measure, and raid just-in-time when Prime Sense detects novelty`.

It does not claim every adopted mechanism is integrated-release-validated on every runtime or that no future donor can improve an implementation.

## Rule

**Saturation changes the search strategy, not the learning posture.**
