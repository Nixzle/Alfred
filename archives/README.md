# Archives

Archives hold reusable knowledge and records. Retrieve only the relevant entry. This public starter deliberately ships without personal research logs, project histories, or conversation-derived records.

Each new entry should record its purpose, principle, procedure, sources, limitations, failure modes, verification, and freshness trigger. Suggested domains: software engineering, product and UX, research, automation, visual production, and release operations. These headings are a filing structure, not claims of existing expertise or research.

Keep project-specific records with their project. Promote only reviewed generalizable lessons through `intake/README.md`; never publish private material just because it was useful during a task.
