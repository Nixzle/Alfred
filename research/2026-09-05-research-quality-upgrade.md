# Research quality and delivery upgrade

Disposition: implemented bounded checks; no new member, service or model version.

Cerebro and Expertise Forge already required source diversity, counterevidence,
information-value prioritisation and just-in-time application. Adding the same
rules again would create documentation without establishing execution quality.
The useful upgrade is explicit measurement scope and failure coverage.

The baseline trace evaluator accepted an empty trace and a research route naming
Cerebro without assessing source quality. New behavior distinguishes NOT_EVALUATED,
PARTIAL and passing recognized checks. Research receipts check provenance fields,
source families, direct evidence versus inference, decision impact and application
results. A separate review is bound to the receipt digest and identified as human
or agent; the checker does not authenticate it or infer semantic truth.

Applying these methods to the runtime identified related Ultron delivery gaps:
an output's implementation/test/deployment level must match the original acceptance
level, and a failed or unknown prerequisite cannot authorize continuing its dependent
operation. The new delivery checker makes these decisions explicit. It must be
invoked; this is not a claim of universal enforcement or model-weight modification.

Validation uses deterministic failure and transfer cases, native Windows isolation
regressions, doctrine lint and signed-task fresh-session recovery. It is not an
unseen research-model benchmark. Meaningful next evidence is semantic calibration
against representative task examples and a scoped live integration, rather than
another broad catalogue of tools.

External grounding: [Anthropic's research evaluation guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
distinguishes task-relative grounding, coverage and source quality. The independent
[Cochrane search handbook](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04)
informs selective source-lineage and search-record practices. Neither establishes
a measured accuracy improvement for this runtime. A recent practitioner-to-platform
route was reused; installing a full evaluation platform was rejected because these
checks can run locally without added infrastructure.

Strongest counterargument: required receipts can become paperwork. Limit them to
material decision-driving claims, preserve explicit unchecked dimensions, and narrow
or retire the procedure if measured task benefit does not justify its cost. Keep
existing theatrical identities and reject a version bump justified only by prose.
